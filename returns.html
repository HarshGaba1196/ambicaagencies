Returns, Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel free of charge any time before the order is dispatched. Message or call us with your order number.
  • After dispatch, the order can’t be cancelled, but you may be able to return it under the rules below.
  • Special-order, made-to-measure or cut-to-size items can’t be cancelled once we have placed the order with the manufacturer or cut the material.

2. Damaged, defective or wrong items

If you receive an item that is damaged in transit, has a manufacturing defect, or is not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos or a video. After checking, we will replace the item free of charge, or refund you if a replacement isn’t available. We will arrange pickup or tell you how to send it back.

3. Changed your mind?

Unused items in their original, undamaged packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery for store credit or a refund. In this case:

  • you return the item to our store, or we deduct the cost of collecting it from your refund;
  • the original delivery charge is not refunded.

4. Items that can’t be returned

  • Items that have been installed, fixed, drilled, cut or used.
  • Made-to-measure, cut-to-size or specially ordered items.
  • Items without their original packaging, parts, screws or accessories.
  • Damage caused by incorrect installation or misuse.

5. Refunds

  • Once we receive and check the returned item, we will tell you whether the refund is approved.
  • Approved refunds are made within 5–7 working days to the original payment method (UPI or bank account). For cash-on-delivery orders, we refund by UPI or bank transfer to an account you choose.
  • Refunds on bulk or project orders may follow the terms of your quotation.

6. Warranty

Many products carry a manufacturer’s warranty. Warranty claims are handled as per the manufacturer’s terms — we will help you with the process.

7. How to request a return

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90000 00000 or email sales@ambicaagencies.in with your order number, the item, the reason, and photos. We usually respond within one working day.
