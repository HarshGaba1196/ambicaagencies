Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Ambica Agencies (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details — your name, mobile number, email (optional), delivery address, city, PIN code and GSTIN (optional), and the products you order.
  • Enquiries — the details you share in our bulk / project enquiry form or over WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Information stored on your device — your cart contents and your light/dark theme choice are saved in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us.

We do not collect card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords through this website.

2. How we use it

  • To confirm, process, deliver and invoice your order, including issuing GST invoices.
  • To contact you about your order, delivery, returns or enquiries.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • To send you offers or updates — only if you agree, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

3. How your order reaches us

When you place an order or enquiry, the website prepares a message containing your details and opens WhatsApp so that you can send it to us. WhatsApp is operated by WhatsApp LLC / Meta, and its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only where needed to serve you:

  • with courier and delivery partners, so they can deliver your order;
  • with payment providers, if you choose to pay online;
  • with government or law-enforcement authorities when required by law.

5. Third-party services on this website

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and some product images from Unsplash. These services may receive your device’s IP address when they deliver that content.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order and invoice records for as long as required under GST and tax laws (generally up to 8 years). Other information is kept only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw any consent you have given, by contacting us using the details below. Some records (such as tax invoices) must be kept by law.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

9. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

10. Grievance officer

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
