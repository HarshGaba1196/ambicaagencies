Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Where we deliver

We currently deliver to [City] and nearby areas. For deliveries to other locations, please contact us before ordering — we will tell you whether we can ship and the cost.

2. Delivery charges

  • Free delivery on orders of ₹2,999 or more.
  • A delivery charge of ₹99 applies to orders below that amount.
  • Heavy, bulky or project orders (for example tall-unit pantries or large quantities) may have extra delivery charges. We will tell you before confirming the order.

3. Dispatch and delivery time

  • In-stock items are usually dispatched within 24–48 hours of order confirmation.
  • Local deliveries usually arrive within 1–3 working days of dispatch. Outstation deliveries usually take 3–7 working days.
  • Items not in stock, or made or cut to size, may take longer — we will tell you the expected time when we confirm your order.
  • Orders are not dispatched on Sundays or public holidays.

4. Store pickup

You can choose “Pick up from store” at checkout. We will message you when your order is ready. Please bring your order number.

5. When your order arrives

Please check the package when it is delivered. If the package is damaged or items are missing, take photos and tell us within 48 hours so that we can help — see our Returns & Refunds Policy.

6. Delays

Delivery dates are estimates. Delays can occasionally happen because of courier issues, weather, strikes or other events outside our control. We will keep you informed.
